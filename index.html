Recovering $7.65M Through Lifecycle Growth Design

Turning cancelled orders into recovered revenue that raised offer acceptance from 10.2% to 44.7%

The problem

$36M in annual sales were at risk.

Cancelled pickup orders were costing $36M a year, and most of those sales could have been saved because the items were available elsewhere.

The strategy

Turn the cancellation into an opportunity.

A lifecycle intervention across email and SMS that turns a dead-end cancellation into an offer to recover the sale.
