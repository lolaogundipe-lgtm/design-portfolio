01 — About the product
What is Live Transcribe?
Live Transcribe captures speech and sound as text for d/Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. It’s Google’s most-used assistive tech, with about 2.5 million daily users.
02 — Problem
Transcription in conversation is awkward
The ergonomic experience breaks down mid-conversation: constant app switching to reply, and frequently turning the device so others can see the screen.

03 — Solution
One device, two screens, no more turning
  • Deployed a text-to-speech experience optimized for dual-screen and foldable devices
  • Inside screen shows the live transcription and keyboard for typing a reply; outside screen mirrors the conversation and typed response for the other person to read
  • Added supporting controls: dual-screen toggle, show/hide text for privacy, voice selection, clear text, and primary/secondary language settings
Inside screen — live transcription, typed reply, and split keyboard.
Outside screen — conversation mirrored for the other person to read.
First-run tour — privacy, speak, and delete controls.
04 — Impact
Shipped with Pixel Fold
The dual-screen feature launched with Pixel Fold / Pixel Pro Fold (Aug 2024), a new take on accessibility across form factors. Live Transcribe became a shared conversational interface, not just a single-user tool.
Pixel Fold — Dual Mode product announcement.
Highlighted in Google’s accessibility launch coverage (Aug 2024).
05 — Research
One device, no hand-offs
Partner research with Samsung showed how hard it is to use Live Transcribe on a single device. Users were juggling phones and handing devices around to keep conversations going.
“When I talk with others, I use Live Transcribe on the Galaxy and insert text on my iPhone to show others. It’s really hard to have a conversation with one device handing here and there.” — Research participant
Research insights
  • Users didn’t want to use multiple devices
  • Users don’t like handing their device to strangers
  • Users were relying on other apps for voice / text-to-speech
06 — Journey
Mapping the dual-screen experience
User journeys and IA work placed the feature where foldable posture made dual-screen mode discoverable — without disrupting core transcription.
Dual Screen journey — start, conversation, typed reply, and spoken response.
IA map — navigation, keyboard controls, and foldable entry points.
07 — Form factors
Considering Samsung Z Flip
Designs covered inside and outside screens, plus a modified version for Samsung Z Flip’s smaller real estate. A delete control let users clear text quickly.

08 — Iteration
From Foldable Mode to Dual Screen
The dual-screen UI evolved across two named versions, then a first-run tooltip tour so icon-only controls still made sense without cluttering the shipped UI.
